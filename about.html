Our little millennium story

Welcome to Little Millennium, Sitapur Road, Lucknow Branch

Located on Sitapur Road, Lucknow, Little Millennium Radha Nagar is dedicated to providing a safe, joyful, and stimulating early learning environment. We believe the early years lay the groundwork for a lifetime of curiosity, confidence, and character. Guided by a scientifically researched, play-based curriculum, our goal is to nurture every child's natural love for discovery while fostering essential cognitive, social, emotional, and motor skills.

Our Core Programs

A progressive pathway for every milestone

01

Developing Roots

Playgroup

Designed for curious toddlers taking their first steps outside home, with sensory exploration, language acquisition, motor skill development, and gentle socialization.

02

Emerging Wings

Nursery

Tailored to build independence, vocabulary, and conceptual understanding through structured play, storytelling, and early numeracy.

03

Ready to Fly 1

Kindergarten 1 / LKG

Focused on foundational literacy, basic mathematical concepts, environmental awareness, problem-solving, and fine motor coordination.

04

Ready to Fly 2

Kindergarten 2 / UKG

Geared toward school-readiness, reading fluency, structured writing, and logical reasoning for a confident transition into primary school.
